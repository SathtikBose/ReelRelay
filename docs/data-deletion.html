ReelRelay Legal Documentation

User Data Deletion Instructions

Last Updated: October 29, 2026
User Right to Deletion: In accordance with Meta Platform Terms and global privacy standards (GDPR, CCPA), ReelRelay provides clear, accessible methods for any Instagram user to request the deletion of all data associated with their account.

1. What Data ReelRelay Stores

ReelRelay only stores minimal technical identifiers necessary to verify deliveries and prevent duplicate automated direct messages:

  • Instagram Public Comment ID
  • Instagram Username or Scoped User ID
  • Matched Keyword and Delivery Timestamp
  • Delivery Status Log (success or failure)

We do not store passwords, profile pictures, personal private chat history, or payment details.

2. Automatic Data Retention & Purging

All activity logs and processed comment records stored in our Cloudflare D1 database are automatically and permanently purged after 60 days. You do not need to take any action if you are satisfied with automatic rolling deletion.

3. How to Request Manual Data Deletion

If you wish to have all stored records associated with your Instagram account deleted immediately, please follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to our data privacy administrator at: sathtikbose@gmail.com
  2. Use the Subject Line: ReelRelay Data Deletion Request
  3. In the body of the email, include your Instagram handle / username (e.g., @yourusername) and approximate date of the comment.

4. Verification & Processing Timeline

Upon receiving your request:

  • Our team will query our Cloudflare D1 database for records matching your Instagram handle or associated comment events.
  • All matching rows in the processed_comments and events_log tables will be permanently deleted.
  • You will receive an email confirmation within 48 hours verifying complete removal.

5. Revoking App Permissions on Instagram

If you previously granted permissions via Meta login, you can revoke access anytime:

  1. Open Instagram on your device or browser and navigate to Settings.
  2. Go to Apps and Websites under your account permissions.
  3. Select active apps and tap Remove next to ReelRelay.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this process, please reach out to:

Administrator: sathtikbose@gmail.com
